Creator Earnings Schedule

Version 1. Effective November 9, 2026

These are the rates Motiv pays creators for hosting collabs. Each collab is paid under the version in effect on the day it starts, and that version applies for the whole collab. Your creator agreement explains when pay is earned, how videos must be delivered, and when you get paid.

How you earn

For each collab, you can earn three types of pay:

  • Recruitment pay is a flat amount based on how many people join.
  • Content pay is a total amount for your weekly and recap videos, paid per video.
  • Performance bonus is a share of Motiv's net revenue from the collab, paid when fewer than 90% of participants finish.

Participants means paying participants. Joining your own collab does not count.

Performance bonus

Paid on every collab, whatever its size, when the win rate is below 90%. The win rate is the percentage of participants who finish the collab, rounded down to the nearest whole percent.

Net revenue = all participants' holds − holds returned to finishers − rewards paid to finishers
Performance bonus = net revenue × your share

If net revenue is zero or less, there is no performance bonus.
